How to use First Merit

Need help finding your way around?

Find quick answers to common questions about creating an account, checking your balance, viewing activity, transfers and getting help.

Getting started
To create an account, select Open Account from the navigation or choose Open an Account from the main page. Complete the signup form and follow the instructions shown on the screen.
Account balance
After signing in, your account dashboard displays your available balance and your main account information. Use the dashboard to review your current account overview.
Account activity
Sign in and go to your dashboard. Look for the account activity or transaction section to review the activity available for your account.
Transfers
Sign in to your account and open the transfer option from your banking dashboard. Enter the required transfer information, review the details carefully, and continue through the confirmation process.
Account access
Go to the Log In page and select Forgot Password. Follow the instructions shown to recover access to your account.
Support
Start with the How to Use section for common questions. For additional assistance, use the support or contact option provided by the application.
