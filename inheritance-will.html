LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT
Of Newman Gordon
I, NEWMAN GORDON, a resident of Sarasota County, State of Florida, being of sound mind and memory, and considering the uncertainty of life, do hereby make, publish, and declare this to be my Last Will and Testament, revoking all prior wills and codicils heretofore made by me.
TESTATOR INFORMATION
Name: Newman Gordon
Age: 67 years old
Residence: Sarasota, Florida
Date of Document: December 2025
Status: Deceased (2026)
First: Debts and Expenses
I direct my Executor to pay all of my just debts, funeral expenses, and the expenses of administering my estate out of my estate, without requiring such debts and expenses to be presented or allowed.
Second: Primary Bequest
I give, devise, and bequeath to BAILEY NEWMAN, my primary beneficiary and designated heir, the sum of TEN MILLION DOLLARS ($10,000,000.00), and all real property located in Sarasota County, Florida, as described herein.
$10,000,000
Beneficiary: Bailey Newman
Property: Bright Realty, 5218 Station Way, Sarasota, FL 34233
Third: Real Property Distribution
The real property known as Bright Realty, located at 5218 Station Way, Sarasota, FL 34233, in Sarasota County, Florida, including all associated real estate holdings, improvements, buildings, and appurtenances thereto, shall be transferred to the designated beneficiary.
Fourth: Appointment of Executor
I hereby nominate, constitute, and appoint Bailey Newman as Executor of this my Last Will and Testament. If Bailey Newman is unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate and appoint First Merit Bank to act as alternate Executor.
Fifth: Powers and Duties
I give to my Executor full power and authority to do all such acts, things, and deeds as shall be deemed necessary or advisable in the collection, management, investment, reinvestment, and distribution of my estate.
Sixth: Residuary Estate
All the rest, residue, and remainder of my estate, both real and personal, I give, devise, and bequeath to Bailey Newman. Should Bailey Newman predecease me, the residuary estate shall pass to their lawful heirs.
Seventh: Compliance with Law
This Will shall be construed and administered according to the laws of the State of Florida. In witness whereof, I have hereunto set my hand to this my Last Will and Testament on this date of execution.
